Computer Phone

A small PhaserJS mini-game: control a phone (📱) to avoid hazards (💣 🧨 ⚡) and collect WiFi signals (📶).

Controls: Arrow keys to move. Collect `📶` to increase score; avoid hazards. Game ends when lives reach 0.

Files:
- `compphoneindex.html`
- `compphone.js`

Inspired by `blatman` emoji-based item usage.
